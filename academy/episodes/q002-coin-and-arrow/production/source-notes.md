## Bloch sphere（Wikipedia，最後編輯 2026-10-25，oldid=1381941389）

- 「The points on the surface of the sphere correspond to the pure states of the system.」
- 「The north and south poles of the Bloch sphere are typically chosen to correspond to the standard basis」，接著寫「This choice is arbitrary, however.」
- |ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + e^{iφ} sin(θ/2)|1⟩，「where 0≤θ≤π and 0≤ϕ<2π.」
- 「with antipodal points corresponding to a pair of mutually orthogonal state vectors.」
- 「Mathematically they are orthogonal even though graphically the angle between them is π.」
- 注意：頁面也寫「The vector with tail at the origin and tip at P is the direction in 3D space corresponding to the spinor.」——對自旋 ½ 粒子，布洛赫向量就是空間中的方向。本集的限制因此只針對超導量子位元。

## Quantum superposition（Wikipedia，oldid=1383426501）

- 「Before the measurement occurs the qubit is in a superposition of both states.」
- 「The probabilities of measuring the system in the |0> or |1> state are given by |c0|2 and |c1|2 respectively.」
- Dirac 引文：「The non-classical nature of the superposition process is brought out clearly.」
